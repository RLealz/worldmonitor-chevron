# Plan 01 - Build Public Compliance Exposure Model

## Goal

Create a deterministic public sanctions/trade exposure model that normalizes screening evidence and connects it to SCM supplier archetype, country, route, and material context without making legal determinations.

## Must Haves

- Exposure output is explicitly a public-data screening signal, not legal advice or final compliance status.
- Every claim carries source/list or source name, timestamp/date or an explicit fallback, confidence, and reason.
- Model accepts existing sanctions pressure, public entity lookup results, trade restrictions/barriers/tariff/flow signals, and Phase 2 supplier archetypes.
- Missing or stale provenance lowers confidence instead of implying clearance or certainty.
- Tests cover sanctions-hit, trade-restricted, combined exposure, stale/missing provenance, and low-coverage scenarios.

## Risks

- Compliance scoring can imply false precision. Keep thresholds simple and explain them through reasons and confidence.
- Trade and sanctions source shapes are uneven. Normalize provenance with explicit fallback labels such as "public source date unavailable".
- Utility imports must preserve dependency direction: types have no imports, utils may import types and existing supplier route helpers only if needed, and fixtures stay in config.
