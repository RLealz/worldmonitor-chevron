# Plan 01 - Build Public Route and Material Context Model

## Goal

Create a deterministic public/demo route and material context model that can power SCM Route Explorer presets, route disruption context, and energy material fallback behavior without implying internal Chevron operational knowledge.

## Must Haves

- Route presets are explicitly public/demo corridors, not Chevron internal routes.
- Presets include origin/destination countries, HS2/product category, cargo type, rationale, public source note, freshness label, confidence, and demo posture.
- Material mappings expose confidence and fallback copy for weak or missing HS/product mappings.
- Context output connects route/chokepoint signals to country, product, material, and public energy SCM signal categories.
- Tests scan the new fixtures/model for private-data-shaped fields and false-precision wording.

## Risks

- Static presets can be mistaken for internal operational lanes. Keep the posture and copy centralized and test for banned phrases.
- Material mappings can overfit broad HS2 categories. Prefer conservative confidence and fallback copy over precise claims.
- New utility imports must preserve architecture direction: types have no imports; config may import types; utils may import types/config and existing public route helpers only.
